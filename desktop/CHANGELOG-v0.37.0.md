# BC2 Cold Wallet v0.42.1

- Desktop-assisted Recovery for 12/24-word BIP39 mnemonics.
- BIP39 word/checksum validation before USB transfer.
- Recovery mnemonic is not persisted or logged by the desktop.
- Hardware independently validates the mnemonic and shows a SHA-256 fingerprint before physical confirmation.
- New-wallet seed creation remains hardware-only.
